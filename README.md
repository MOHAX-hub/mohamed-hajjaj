## About Me

I’m Mohamed Hajjaj, a self-taught technology enthusiast from Morocco with a passion for programming, robotics, electronics, embedded systems, and the Internet of Things.

Since childhood, I have been curious about cars, airplanes, and how technology works. After completing my Baccalaureate, I continued learning independently through practical experimentation, online resources, and personal projects. I started with Arduino and gradually explored robotics, IoT, electronics, and programming.

I believe in learning by building real things, making mistakes, and improving continuously. My goal is to turn ideas into useful prototypes and keep developing my engineering skills.

### My journey

I am a person with a disability. It is one part of my life, but it does not define my abilities, creativity, or ambitions. Some hands-on hardware tasks can present physical challenges; I continue to explore technology through approaches that work for me. This experience has also strengthened my interest in accessible technology and solutions that make everyday life easier. I prefer to keep the focus on learning, projects, and what I can build.
